Price from Camarilla S2/S4/R2/R4 • direction from RSI 21 (40/60) • analysis only, never places orders

🔬 Analysis📈 RSI Analysis🧪 Strategy Lab📊 Reports← Dashboard
BUY (ATM CE): RSI goes below 40 and closes back above 40, and the candle is at S2 or S4. Stop = candle low. Target = next line up (S4→S2, S2→R2).
SELL (ATM PE): RSI goes above 60 and closes back below 60, and the candle is at R2 or R4. Stop = candle high. Target = next line down (R4→R2, R2→S2).
No trade when a buy signal comes at resistance, a sell signal at support, price is "in the air" (no level touched), or the stop is too wide. Pivots come from the previous day's high/low/close.
