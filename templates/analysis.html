How this test works. It replays the bot's own rules on stored 1-minute candles: a buy at the start of every slot at that minute's open price, its own target per entry (rounded up to the tick size), no stop loss, positions held overnight, plus your max open entries, capital and batch limits. A sale happens at the target price, or at the open if the price gaps above it. When 1-minute data can't tell which happened first, the test assumes the less favourable order. Real fills can differ by a tick or two, and brokerage is only included through the charges % you enter. Past results don't guarantee future results.
