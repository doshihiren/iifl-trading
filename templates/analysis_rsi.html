How this test works. Same rules as the bot (a buy at the start of every slot at that minute's open, its own target per entry, no stop loss, positions held overnight, max open / capital / batch limits), plus RSI. RSI is Wilder RSI on 1-minute closes. Every decision uses the RSI of the candle that had already closed – nothing from the future. Signals raised at a candle's close are executed at the next candle's open. Dip rules: the ladder lets the first lots buy freely, then each extra lot must be X% below the lowest open lot; the trend filter compares the last close with the average of previous days' closes; volume rules compare a 1-minute candle's volume with the 20 candles before it. A ridden lot's target becomes its floor; it is sold at the RSI peak, when RSI fades, at the floor, or at the max stretch. The first ~10 days before your start date are loaded only to warm up RSI. When 1-minute data can't tell which happened first, the less favourable order is assumed. Real fills can differ by a tick or two. Past results don't guarantee future results.
