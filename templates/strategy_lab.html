Trades

How the Lab tests. Signals are decided when a candle of the chosen timeframe closes and filled at the next minute's open. Stops and targets are checked every minute. A price gap fills at the open, and if one minute touches both stop and target it assumes the stop came first. Charges use approximate NSE rates (delivery STT 0.1% each side; intraday STT 0.025% on sell; stamp duty, exchange and SEBI fees, 18% GST) plus your brokerage. Check them against your contract note. Real fills can be worse, especially with big orders, so keep each order under the "safe order size" above. Past results don't guarantee future results, and this is not investment advice.
