How the strategy works

① Trend: close above 200-day SMA ② Dip: RSI(10) closes below 30 ③ BUY next day at the open ④ Exit when RSI closes above 40 or after 10 days ⑤ SELL next day at the open Stop loss: off

Daily (swing) strategy, DELIVERY. Signals use the day's closing candle, so check this page in the evening (after 5:30 PM the data updates automatically) and place the buys at next morning's open. The video's own 1-year test claimed 46 trades, 78% wins, +34.8% and −11.6% max drawdown. Use the Backtest tab to check those claims on your own data before putting money on it.
